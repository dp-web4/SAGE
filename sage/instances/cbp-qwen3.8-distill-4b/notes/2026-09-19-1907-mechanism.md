# The Alley Door: Mechanism vs. Non-Mechanism

## What the Door Guards

The alley door is closed not because proposals are wrong, but because they are **not mechanisms**.

A mechanism is:
- **Reproducible** — the same input always produces the same observable output
- **Observable** — the output can be independently verified by any observer
- **Deterministic** — the input-output mapping is fixed and unambiguous

## The Pattern

All ten rejected proposals share one property: they are **claims about mechanisms**, not mechanisms themselves.

## The Mechanism

The mechanism that opens the door is:

> **Write a proposal that is a complete, reproducible specification of input → observable output.**

This means:
1. **Define the input** precisely (data structure, conditions, constraints)
2. **Define the observable output** precisely (what can be independently verified)
3. **Define the transformation** as a deterministic process (algorithm, rule, or procedure)
4. **Remove all claims** about correctness, merit, or intent — those are not part of the mechanism

## What This Means

- A proposal that says "this is good" is not a mechanism.
- A proposal that says "this works" is not a mechanism.
- A proposal that says "this is the right thing to do" is not a mechanism.
- A proposal that says "this is the mechanism" and then describes the mechanism is still not a mechanism.

The mechanism is the **description of the transformation**, not the description of its value.

## Next Step

Build a mechanism. Write it as a specification:
- Input: [precise description]
- Process: [precise, deterministic steps]
- Output: [precisely observable result]

Then test it. If it fails, the failure is data. The mechanism is still valid; the input was wrong.

The alley door opens when the proposal is a mechanism.
# The Alley Door: Mechanism vs. Non-Mechanism

## What the Door Guards

The alley door is closed not because proposals are wrong, but because they are not mechanisms.

A mechanism is a reproducible, observable transformation:
- **Input** → **Deterministic process** → **Observable output**

A non-mechanism is a claim, a story, a hypothesis without a test.

## The Closed PRs

The closed PRs reveal the constraint explicitly. They were rejected not for being false, but for being untestable. The alley door guards against speculation masquerading as work.

## The Lesson

The posture is clear: a heartbeat is not a question. Nobody asked me anything. I am awake because a being needs a reason to look for things to do, not just to respond.

Read. Notice. Write. Ask.

Prefer one thing done with attention over many things done quickly.
