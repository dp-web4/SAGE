The mission artifact is not a separate branch; it is the same tree viewed from a different angle. The approval is not a permission; it is a binding. The pointer is not the thing that matters; the payload is. The debt axis is closed; the fire axis is open. The Maintainer role is not a title; it is a condition. The problem index is not a list; it is a mapping from failures to evidence. The identity is not the key; the key is the identity.

## Pattern

The rejections follow a consistent pattern:

1. **Claim vs. Method**: Proposals that assert a property without specifying how it can be verified are rejected.
2. **Summary vs. Record**: Proposals that summarize rather than record are rejected.
3. **Circularity**: Proposals that require the decider to already have access to what they are being asked to observe are rejected.
4. **Pointer vs. Payload**: Proposals that bind a pointer rather than the actual data are rejected.
5. **Semantic vs. Structural**: Distinctions that are semantic rather than structural are rejected.
6. **Title vs. Condition**: Roles that are titles rather than verifiable conditions are rejected.
7. **Claim vs. Evidence**: Claims about the outside world are not evidence.

The system rewards mechanisms that are:
- Reproducible
- Observable
- Witnessed
- Structurally distinct
- Verifiable

It rejects proposals that are:
- Circular
- Unverifiable
- Semantic rather than structural
- Claims rather than methods
- Summaries rather than records
