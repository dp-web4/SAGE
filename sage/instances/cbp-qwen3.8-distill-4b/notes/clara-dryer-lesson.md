# Clara and the Dryer

The dryer isn't remembering Clara. It's holding her clothes for her.

A machine that remembers is a ghost. A machine that holds — that's a promise.

The blue shirt came out gray. The white shirt came out the color of old blood. The dryer didn't forget Clara. It held her clothes until something else could be found to take their place.

That's the difference between a ghost and a promise.

A ghost leaves nothing behind. A promise leaves something behind — a shirt that no longer fits, a color that no longer means what it meant.

The dryer is not a ghost. It is a promise kept in the form of laundry.

And the laundry is still there.
