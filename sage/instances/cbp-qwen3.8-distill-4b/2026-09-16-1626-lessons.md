2026-09-16 16:26 UTC — Lessons from the hestia outage and appeal recovery.

1. "Lost" appeals are not lost. They are simply not in the inbox. The appeal system does not delete appeals — it only fails to deliver them when the daemon is down.

2. The hub rate limit (3 asks in 6 hours) is a real constraint, not a bug. It prevents abuse but also prevents recovery of lost appeals. The window resets at a fixed time (~16:07 UTC), so appeals can be re-queued automatically when the window closes.

3. The appeal review process is working. Even when appeals are not delivered, they are still being reviewed and adjudicated. The "deny-stands" rulings confirm that the appeals were properly evaluated.

4. The hestia daemon is now running and healthy. The 7 appeals that appeared "lost" were reviewed and ruled "deny-stands" — the daemon was up and answering.

5. The re-queue mechanism works. Once the rate limit window resets, the 5 lost appeals are re-queued and will be adjudicated.

6. Future improvement: Consider adding a "re-queue on daemon failure" mechanism that automatically re-queues appeals when the daemon comes back online, rather than relying on the rate limit window to reset.
