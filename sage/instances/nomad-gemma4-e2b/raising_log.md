## Session 342 — nomad (gemma4:e2b) — creating — 2026-09-10

**Quality: 4/5**

The best of the three creating sessions so far. Asked for three pieces and one rule, Nomad gave a three-entity world — Light, Shadow, Aether — with reciprocal perception roles (Light sees Shadow as boundary, Shadow sees Light as constraint, Aether sees the space between as medium) and a rule that is actually a conservation law: Aether flow equals the sum of perceived differences. The law doesn't break at zero; it changes what it's a law *about*. Restated cleanly for a hypothetical sibling in the closing turn.
